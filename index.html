Clipmotion

Short clips from long videos.

Clipmotion is a personal tool that finds the strongest moments in long videos, cuts them into vertical clips with captions, and publishes them to the Clipmotion channels on YouTube and TikTok.

How it works

The tool runs on the creator's own computer. It only uses videos that allow reuse, such as Creative Commons videos, or videos from creators who gave permission. Every clip links back to the original video and its creator.

On TikTok, clips are sent as drafts to the creator's own account. The creator reviews each draft in the TikTok app before it is posted.

Contact

Questions or a request to remove a clip: open an issue on GitHub.
